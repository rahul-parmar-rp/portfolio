# Health Tracker App

## Overview

This app allows users to track their daily health metrics, including exercise, diet, and sleep patterns, promoting a healthier lifestyle.

## Features

- **Daily Logging**: Users can log their meals, workouts, and sleep.
- **Progress Tracking**: Visual representation of progress towards health goals.
- **Reminders**: Customizable reminders for hydration, meals, and workouts.

## Use Cases

- Suitable for fitness enthusiasts who want to monitor their routines.
- Helpful for individuals looking to improve their overall health and wellness.

## Additional Notes

- Ensure data privacy and security measures are in place for user health information.
